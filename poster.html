Key Finding

Maharashtra's monsoon gradient collapses from 3,500 mm on the Konkan coast to 550 mm just 80 km east — MahaRainRisk resolves drought risk at the sub-district level, delivering university-backed advisories in Marathi & English before crop loss, not after.

01 · The Problem

Over 80% of Maharashtra's cropland is rainfed. National-scale models aggregate rainfall across hundreds of kilometres, masking the extreme sub-regional gradients that define whether a taluka faces catastrophic loss or a normal harvest.

A 30 % deficit in Solapur (normal 550 mm) is a disaster. The same departure in Mahabaleshwar (normal 3,500 mm) is trivial. Crop decisions are enacted at the taluka level. Models must match.
